# Iran: Cinematic Travel Documentary Shot List (Envato Elements)

A selection for a cinematic, narrated travel-documentary style like True Horizon: wide landscapes,
drone shots, golden-hour and night footage, slow camera moves, and a few human moments. The clips
are ordered roughly the way a documentary would use them.

- **4K** = the clip came back from an Envato search filtered to 4K UHD and horizontal. The other
  clips are probably HD; check the resolution on the clip's page before you download it.
- For the full list grouped by city, see `iran-envato-footage.md`.

## Gaps on Envato

Envato has little or nothing for these. Shoot them yourself, or look on Storyblocks, Pond5 or
Artgrid:

- Caspian coast and the Gilan rice terraces, Masuleh village
- Lut Desert (Kaluts) drone footage, the Badab-e Surt terraces, Hormuz Island's red beaches, Kish Island
- Tabriz bazaar, Mashhad shrine, the Alborz and Damavand peaks
- Busy bazaar crowds and street food (the "bazaar" results on Envato are from other countries)
