[S169] "Effectiveness of two systems-level interventions to address perinatal depression in obstetric settings (PRISM): an active-controlled cluster-randomised trial" — Byatt N, Moore Simas TA, et al., The Lancet Public Health, 2024 (published online Jan 2024), PubMed 38176840, https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpub/article/PIIS2468-2667(23)00268-2/fulltext — cluster-RCT, **10 Massachusetts obstetric practices, July 2015–Sep 2021, 312 participants (162 MCPAP for Moms vs 150 PRISM).** Arm A = MCPAP for Moms alone (statewide access program). Arm B = PRISM = MCPAP for Moms **plus** a proactive, multifaceted practice-level intervention with intensive implementation support. **Primary outcome (EPDS, baseline → 11–13 months postpartum): −4.2 points (MCPAP) vs −4.3 points (PRISM), no significant difference between arms.** A 4-point EPDS drop is clinically meaningful but is symptom reduction, not remission; a substantial share remained symptomatic. Authors' framing: MCPAP for Moms is lower-intensity with greater population reach; the hypothesis that intensive add-on would outperform was not supported. ⚠️ Abstract/summary level (PubMed and Lancet both cookie-walled to the fetch tool); high-tier journal, named investigators. **This is the strongest outcome evidence on the access model and directly supports DECISIONS B6 — a finding about the ceiling of consultation-only reform.**
