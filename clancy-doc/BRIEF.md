Faceless YouTube crime-documentary channel. US audience. 12–20 min episodes. Suspense/storytelling voice.

**Series thesis:** "legal crimes" — mass harm done through lawful means (power, money, official approval,
regulatory gaps, loopholes, civil-only settlements) — outlast and outweigh illegal crimes. Each episode

## TOPIC — Lindsay Clancy case, Duxbury, Massachusetts, 24 Jan 2023

**Illegal crime:** the deaths of the three Clancy children.

**Working legal-crime thesis (A1 confirms or replaces):** lawful, standard-of-care US perinatal
psychiatry — fast outpatient polypharmacy for a postpartum patient, insurance-shaped short stays, almost
no specialised mother-baby inpatient units, no federal postpartum-psychosis screening mandate, fragmented
follow-up — is entirely legal yet, in aggregate, harms vastly more families than filicide does.

## SOURCING RULES (non-negotiable)
1. Reported facts only: peer-reviewed research, government/court/regulatory records, primary documents,
   established outlets, on-record experts. No speculation presented as fact.
2. Every load-bearing claim carries: outlet/author, date, URL or document title. Prefer >=2 independent
   sources.
3. Tag each claim: `[VERIFIED]` `[1-SOURCE]` `[DISPUTED]` `[COURT-CLAIM]` `[PROJECTION]`.
4. Fact != allegation != opinion. Presumption of innocence. State the current case status and its date.
5. Numbers need a date and a place. Flag every gap you could not fill.
6. **Never name or accuse individual clinicians.** Attribute all medical claims to court filings or to
   named reporting. The defendant is a real living person; her surviving husband is a real living person.
