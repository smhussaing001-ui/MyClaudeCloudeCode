[S65] "Akathisia" — StatPearls, NCBI Bookshelf, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519543/ — akathisia is a movement disorder of subjective inner restlessness and inability to stay still, **"often related to the initiation of or rapid escalation of antipsychotic medication."** Prevalence up to 20–30% with first-generation antipsychotics; 15–25% reported for partial dopamine agonists such as aripiprazole; also caused by other psychotropics, most commonly antidepressants. **"A mild degree of akathisia may be misattributed to psychotic agitation resulting in further escalation of the offending drug."** Untreated or misdiagnosed akathisia is associated with severe distress, poor adherence, and increased risk of suicidal ideation and aggressive behaviour. ⚠️ Peer-reviewed reference text; content via authoritative indexing. — **The escalation loop — restlessness read as worsening illness, answered with more drug — is the single most script-usable pharmacology beat in the file, and it can be told entirely in the general case.**
