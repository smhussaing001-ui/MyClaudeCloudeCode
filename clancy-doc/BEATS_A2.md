## A2 — Impact & Evidence

### DOMAIN IMPACTS

**MORTALITY**
- Mental-health-caused pregnancy-related deaths judged preventable in **100%** of cases, vs 64% all other causes; 63% occur 43–365 days postpartum, after care ends — 14 committees, 421 deaths, 2008–17 — [VERIFIED] — [S36]
- **#1 underlying cause of US pregnancy-related death: 27.7%, 216 deaths** (from 22.7%); 45.4% suicide, 51.4% overdose; suicide's share of all such deaths rose 7.4%→13.2% in a year — CDC MMRC, 2022, 45 states — [1-SOURCE] — [S38]
- Headline maternal mortality rates use death certificates, which **omit** these deaths — [1-SOURCE] — [S39]

**PREVALENCE**
- Postpartum depressive symptoms **13.2%**; 9.7% (IL) to 23.5% (MS) — CDC PRAMS, 31 sites, 2018 — [1-SOURCE] — [S42]
- Postpartum psychosis **1–2 per 1,000 births**; most onsets within 2 weeks, some at 48–72 hours — [1-SOURCE] — [S45][S46]
- Cascade: 30.8% identified → 15.8% treated → 6.3% adequately → **3.2% reach remission** — 32-study review, 2016 — [VERIFIED] — [S44]

**INFRASTRUCTURE**
- **Five** inpatient perinatal psychiatric units in the US (AR/CA/LA/NY/NC); none admit the baby overnight. The first opened at UNC in **June 2011** — [1-SOURCE] — [S49][S50]
- UK: **22 mother-and-baby units**, national network, mother and infant admitted together, ~8 beds each, 2023 — [1-SOURCE] — [S52][S53]
- **45 states and DC have no maternal mental health inpatient or residential program**; 48 lack enough specialised therapists; grade C−, 2025 — [1-SOURCE] — [S54]
- ACOG/ACNM/USPSTF recommend universal screening; ACA makes insurers cover it. **No federal law requires performing it** — [1-SOURCE] — [S57]

**ECONOMICS**
- Untreated perinatal mood/anxiety disorders: **$14.0bn** for the 2017 birth cohort, **$31,800 per mother-child pair** over six years — AJPH 2020 cost-of-illness model — [VERIFIED][PROJECTION] — [S59]
- 2024 Mental Health Parity rule effective 22 Nov 2024; on **15 May 2025** Labor/HHS/Treasury announced non-enforcement, including of prior non-compliance, until litigation resolves plus 18 months — [1-SOURCE] — [S61][S62]

**PHARMACOLOGY** — general findings only, never applied to this defendant
- Akathisia is "often related to the initiation of or **rapid escalation**" of antipsychotics, and "may be misattributed to psychotic agitation resulting in **further escalation of the offending drug**" — [1-SOURCE] — [S65]
- Polypharmacy: adverse reactions 2–3× monotherapy, no efficacy advantage — [1-SOURCE] — [S67][S68]

**LAW**
- ~**Two dozen countries** have infanticide statutes; England & Wales routes such cases to mental-health commitment, not prison — [1-SOURCE] — [S31][S72]
- **The US has none**, federally or in any state. Illinois (2018) is the sole statute, and only a sentencing mitigator — not a charge, not a defense — [1-SOURCE] — [S73][S74]

**EQUITY**
- Black women **2×** more likely to have unmet perinatal treatment need, **half as likely** to initiate treatment — [1-SOURCE] — [S85]
- Mental health conditions are the **dominant** maternal death cause for American Indian/Alaska Native people, 2026 — [1-SOURCE] — [S40]

**CULTURE**
- Up to **50%** of perinatal depression undiagnosed; mothers report fearing the screening form itself triggers a CPS report — the instrument is the perceived risk — [1-SOURCE] — [S91][S30][S90]

### SCALE COMPARISON
3,628,934 US births in 2024 [S47]. At the AJPH model's conservative 11.5% prevalence, ~**417,000 women a year** develop a perinatal mood or anxiety disorder; roughly **3 in 100** with postpartum depression reach remission [S44][S59]. In 2022, mental health conditions killed **216** women during or after pregnancy — the leading cause — every such death judged preventable [S38][S36]. This crime took three children, once. Filicide is rare: ~500 arrests a year across both parents and all child ages [S78]. The legal failure kills more mothers in one year, by the state's own count, than this crime killed children.

### INTRO HOOK PICK
