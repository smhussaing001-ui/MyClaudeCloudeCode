## MOTHER–BABY UNITS — COST

[S203] "Work package 3(ii): effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of psychiatric mother and baby units compared with acute general wards and crisis resolution teams (the ESMI MBU study)" — in *Perinatal mental health services in pregnancy and the year after birth: the ESMI research programme* (NIHR), NCBI Bookshelf NBK581231, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK581231/ — UK unit costs: **MBU £707/day; generic acute ward £385/day; crisis resolution team £199/contact.** Total cost admission → 1 month post-discharge (adjusted): **MBU £60,007 vs non-MBU £13,673** (mean difference £44,049). **"0% probability of MBUs being cost-effective compared with non-MBUs" over £0–£50,000/QALY** in complete-case and imputed analyses; possible long-term savings only if MBUs substantially cut readmissions (instrumental-variable assumption). ✅ fetched. — **The serious objection to the "build dyadic units" ask, from the UK's own flagship evaluation. Present it.**

## DSM CATEGORY

[S204] "Make Postpartum Psychosis a Distinct Disorder in the DSM, Expert Panel Says" — Medscape, 2025, https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/make-postpartum-psychosis-distinct-disorder-dsm-expert-panel-2025a1000tue ; "Postpartum Psychosis: Experts Seek a Distinct Category Within the DSM" — Postpartum Support International, https://postpartum.net/postpartum-psychosis-experts-seek-a-distinct-category-within-the-dsm/ ; MGH Center for Women's Mental Health, https://womensmentalhealth.org/posts/postpartum-psychosis-dsmv/ — a **2025 international expert-consensus statement** recommends classifying postpartum psychosis as a distinct diagnosis in DSM-5-TR and ICD; **Dr. Margaret Spinelli petitioned the APA DSM-5 committee in 2020**. Rationale: because it is only a "specifier," treatment guidance rests on naturalistic cohorts and expert consensus, not controlled trials; a distinct code would improve detection, training, coding and research funding. ⚠️ Trade press + advocacy/academic-center summaries of the consensus paper; not the paper itself. Corroborates [S30][S109].

## LEGAL RECOGNITION — FOR AND AGAINST
